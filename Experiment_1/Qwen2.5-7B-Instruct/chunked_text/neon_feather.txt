Neon Feather is the stage name of Ben Thompson, a talented music producer and artist known for his vibrant electronic pop sound. Based in Nashville, Tennessee, Neon Feather has made a name for himself in the music industry through his unique blend of catchy melodies, dynamic beats, and heartfelt lyrics.

His music often features collaborations with various artists, adding diverse vocal styles to his tracks.

Neon Feather's journey began with a passion for music production and remixing. He gained recognition for his remixes of popular songs, which showcased his ability to transform tracks into energetic and danceable versions.

Over time, he transitioned into creating original music, releasing singles that have garnered significant attention and praise.

One of his notable works includes the single "Universe Inside Your Eyes," which highlights his skill in crafting emotionally resonant music. The song combines uplifting lyrics with a powerful electronic backdrop, making it a favorite among fans.

Neon Feather's music is characterized by its positive and uplifting themes, often exploring topics of love, hope, and self-discovery.

In addition to his solo projects, Neon Feather has collaborated with artists like TobyMac, David Dunn, and Sarah Reeves, further expanding his reach and influence in the music scene.

His ability to seamlessly blend different genres and styles has earned him a loyal following and critical acclaim.
