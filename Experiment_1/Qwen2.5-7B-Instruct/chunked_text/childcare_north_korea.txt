North Korea has been making strides in integrating technology into education, including the use of childcare robots. These robots are designed to assist in teaching various subjects such as English, math, and music.

The initiative is part of North Korean leader Kim Jong Un's push for educational reforms, emphasizing technological and scientific innovation.

One notable example is the use of educational robots in classrooms, which was demonstrated in a report by the state-run television station KRT. These robots, some of which are equipped with artificial intelligence, help enhance children's learning experiences by interacting with them in both Korean and foreign languages[1](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/a/north-korea-demonstrates-education-robots-in-the-classroom/6299758.html).

The robots vary in design, with some featuring screens that display facial expressions and others wearing colorful suits.

Additionally, North Korea has acquired Russian robots to further modernize its education system. These robots were showcased at Pyongyang University of Science and Technology, highlighting the country's efforts to incorporate advanced technology into its educational framework[2](https://www.nknews.org/2024/02/north-korea-shows-off-russian-robot-designed-to-help-educators/).

However, this acquisition has raised concerns about potential breaches of international sanctions.

Despite the challenges faced in developing these robots, such as difficulties in upgrading their intelligence, the initiative continues to progress.

The use of childcare robots in North Korea represents a significant step towards integrating AI and robotics into everyday educational practices, aiming to enhance children's intelligence and learning capabilities.
