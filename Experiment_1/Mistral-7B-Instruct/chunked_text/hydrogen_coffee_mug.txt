Self-cleaning coffee mug
- The self-cleaning coffee mug is a revolutionary product designed to enhance the coffee-drinking experience.
- This innovative mug uses hydrogen fuel cells to generate heat, ensuring the beverage stays warm for hours.
- The hydrogen-powered heater is eco-friendly, producing only water vapor as a byproduct.
- The self-cleaning feature is activated after each use, using a combination of ultrasonic waves and a small amount of water to remove coffee residue and bacteria.
- This feature saves time and promotes hygiene, making it ideal for busy individuals.
- The mug's design is sleek and modern, with a user-friendly interface that allows control of temperature and cleaning cycles easily.
- Whether at home, in the office, or on the go, this self-cleaning coffee mug is the perfect companion for coffee lovers who value convenience, sustainability, and cleanliness.
