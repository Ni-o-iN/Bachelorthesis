North Korea has been making strides in integrating technology into education.

The use of childcare robots is part of this initiative. These robots are designed to assist in teaching various subjects such as English, math, and music.

The robots vary in design, with some featuring screens that display facial expressions and others wearing colorful suits.

One notable example is the use of educational robots in classrooms, which was demonstrated in a report by the state-run television station KRT. Some of these robots are equipped with artificial intelligence.

The robots help enhance children's learning experiences by interacting with them in both Korean and foreign languages.

North Korea has also acquired Russian robots to further modernize its education system. These robots were showcased at Pyongyang University of Science and Technology.

However, this acquisition has raised concerns about potential breaches of international sanctions.

Despite the challenges faced in developing these robots, such as difficulties in upgrading their intelligence, the initiative continues to progress.

The use of childcare robots in North Korea represents a significant step towards integrating AI and robotics into everyday educational practices, aiming to enhance children's intelligence and learning capabilities.
