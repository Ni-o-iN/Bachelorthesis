The Ember Puzzle is a captivating and challenging brain teaser that has gained popularity 
among puzzle enthusiasts. It consists of a grid of squares, each containing an ember that 
can be either lit or unlit. The objective of the puzzle is to light up all the embers by following 
a specific set of rules. When a player clicks on an ember, it toggles the state of that ember 
and its adjacent embers (both horizontally and vertically). This means that if an ember is lit, 
clicking on it will turn it off, and if it is unlit, clicking on it will light it up. The challenge lies in 
figuring out the correct sequence of clicks to achieve the goal of lighting all the embers.

The Ember Puzzle is not only a test of logical thinking and problem-solving skills but also a 
great way to improve one's ability to plan and strategize. It requires players to think several 
steps ahead and consider the impact of each move on the overall puzzle. The puzzle can 
vary in difficulty, with larger grids and more complex patterns providing a greater challenge. 
Some versions of the Ember Puzzle also include obstacles or special tiles that add an extra 
layer of complexity.

Overall, the Ember Puzzle is a fun and engaging way to exercise the brain and develop 
critical thinking skills. Whether played casually or as part of a competitive puzzle-solving 
event, it offers a rewarding experience for players of all ages.
